# Privacy Policy for Shorts Speed Control

**Last Updated:** January 29, 2026

## Personal Data Collection
Shorts Speed Control does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension does not track browsing history, user activity, or usage analytics, and does not identify users in any way.

## Data Usage
The extension uses `chrome.storage.local` exclusively to store user preferences required for its functionality, including:
- Selected playback speed.
- Enabled or disabled state of the extension.
- Interface-related state (for example, whether an informational tooltip has been shown).

All preference data is stored locally on the user's device and is never transmitted to external servers, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than providing the extension’s core functionality.

## Network and Third-Party Access
The extension does not communicate with external servers, does not load remote code, and does not integrate with third-party services or analytics providers.

## Changes to This Policy
If this privacy policy is updated, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be revised accordingly.
