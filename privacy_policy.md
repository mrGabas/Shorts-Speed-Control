# Privacy Policy for Shorts Speed Control

**Last Updated:** January 19, 2026

## Personal Data Collection
This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not track your browsing history or usage of the extension.

## Data Usage
The extension uses `chrome.storage.local` solely to store your user preferences, specifically:
- Your selected playback speed.
- Whether the extension is enabled or disabled.
- Interface state (e.g., whether a tooltip has been shown).

This data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with any third parties or external servers.
